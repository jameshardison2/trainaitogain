Why TrainAIToGain Exists

"I took the AI screening interviews myself. Here's why I made this free."

When I first took AI screening interviews (like Mercor and micro1's Zara), I saw how frustratingly easy it is for world-class engineers, physicians, and finance pros to get rejected over subtle things: unnatural pauses, missing ATS keywords, or speaking without measurable metrics.

I created TrainAIToGain to share the exact keyword rubrics and conversational pacing needed to pass. This service is 100% free for candidates.
