⚠️ Pre-Apply Checklist: 4 Costly Mistakes to Avoid Before Submitting

These are the top avoidable disqualifications candidates report in applicant forums. Verify each item before clicking apply:

1. Match Profile Availability to the Role’s Requirements (Only If You Can Commit)
Check the schedule and hours listed on the role card before applying. If a role specifies 20 hrs/wk, setting your profile to 10 hours can filter your application out of consideration. Align your profile availability with the role's required hours, but only if you can actually commit to working them once placed.
Application Strategy • Avoid availability mismatches
2. Apply Early & Complete 100% of Profile in One Sitting
Partially filled profiles get deprioritized in hiring manager reviews (HMR). Complete all portfolio links, resume parsing, and baseline questions in your first session.
Source: micro1 HMR Guidelines • mercor hiring queue
3. Start Tasks Promptly Once Placed on a Project
When matched to a project wave, claim and submit your initial task right away. Accounts with 0 tasks completed risk having their seat reassigned to standby evaluators.
Source: Contractor Project Allocation Guidelines
4. Confirm Country Eligibility & Deel Tax Status
Check whether the role requires US residency (W-9) or accepts International contractors (W-8BEN). Both platforms disburse earnings via Deel; verify your documents early.
Source: Deel Contractor Onboarding Documentation
💻 1. Hardware & System Setup
Desktop or Laptop Computer Only (No Phones or Tablets)
Mercor does not support mobile browsers. Full-screen sharing is required throughout the assessment.
Source: Mercor Candidate FAQ (work.mercor.com)
Google Chrome Browser (Updated to Latest Version)
Chrome provides the most reliable WebRTC audio/video and screen share pipelines. Google Chrome is strongly recommended for optimal screening stability.
Source: Mercor & micro1 Technical Requirements
Wired Headset / Earphones with Dedicated Microphone
Built-in laptop microphones pick up room echo and keyboard clicks, confusing automated speech-to-text parsers. Use wired headphones with a mic.
Source: TrainAIToGain Audio Transcription Analysis
High-Speed, Stable Internet (Minimum 15 Mbps Download / 5 Mbps Upload)
Close streaming apps, VPNs, downloads, and heavy background tabs before starting to prevent video stutter or audio drops.
Source: Mercor System Check Requirements
💡 2. Physical Space & Lighting
Front-Facing Light Source (No Backlighting)
Position light or a window directly in front of your face. Windows behind you cast you in shadow and trigger automated lighting warnings.
Source: Computer Vision Facial Landmark Guidelines
Quiet, Closed Room with Zero Distractions
Notify household members and close windows. AI screeners penalize background human voices as potential collaboration or cheating.
Source: Mercor Honor Code & Proctoring Rules
20 Uninterrupted Minutes Scheduled
Mute phone, silence computer Slack/Discord notifications, and close secondary communication windows.
Source: Mercor Candidate Walkthrough
⚖️ 3. Platform Rules & Retake Policies
Mercor Practice Interviews
Mercor offers practice interviews on its platform that are not shared with hiring managers. Test your audio and pacing there before your screening.
Source: Mercor Official Candidate Portal (work.mercor.com)
Prepare Before Official Screening Submission
Official screening interviews attach directly to your candidate profile for client evaluation. Complete a practice interview first to make sure your setup and pacing are solid.
Source: Mercor Candidate Portal (work.mercor.com)
micro1: Target One Core Skill & Use Zara Prep
On micro1, specialize your profile in one primary tech stack or evaluation domain. Use the micro1 prep tool to familiarize yourself with Zara's evaluation rubrics.
Source: micro1 AI Recruiter Guidelines (micro1.ai)
🎯 4. Answer Structure & Pacing Formula
Target Answer Duration: 60 to 90 Seconds
Responses under 30 seconds lack sufficient keyword signals for the transcription scorer. Responses over 2 minutes risk rambling. Aim for 70–120 words per question.
Source: TrainAIToGain Rubric Analysis
Strict STAR Framework: Situation, Task, Action, Result
State the challenge (10s), your assigned task (10s), the exact actions you owned (30s), and end with a quantified, measurable outcome (15s).
Source: Industry Standard AI Evaluation Benchmark
Prepare 3 Quantified Stories From Your Resume
Have numbers ready: % reduction in latency, dollars saved, patient volumes handled, or accuracy benchmarks achieved. AI screeners weight concrete metrics heavily.
Source: Mercor Technical Rubric Criteria
Look at the Camera Lens, Do Not Read From a Hidden Script
Eye-tracking algorithms flag candidates reading pre-written notes on secondary screens. Natural pauses and eye contact with the webcam build credibility.
Source: AI Interview Proctoring Analysis
