How To Use This Platform

TrainAIToGain is a 3-step engine designed to prep you for the AI grading algorithms.

1

Scan Your Resume

Use the ATS Scanner on Step 1 of the Hiring Pipeline. The AI parser must be able to read your skills, or you will be automatically rejected. Fix any missing keywords before applying.

2

Mock The AI Interview

You will be interviewed by a machine, not a human. Use our AI Mock Interview on Step 2 to practice the STAR method and get comfortable answering to a camera without stuttering.

3

Apply via the Index

Once you are prepped, head to the Opportunities page. Find your specific domain (Software, Medical, Legal) and click Apply Now to enter the portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a PDF resume?

No! Both PDF and DOCX formats are perfectly fine. The most important thing is that your resume contains the exact keywords relevant to the role.

How much does this pay?

Rates vary wildly depending on your niche expertise, but the highest-tier technical, medical, and legal roles cap out around $100-160/hr. Generalist roles will be significantly lower.

Is the video interview live?

No, it is an asynchronous one-way interview. An AI avatar will ask you questions, and you will record your answers. The AI will then transcribe and grade your responses.

The "Keyword Drop" Strategy

Do not just list skills at the bottom of your resume. The AI gives higher weight to skills mentioned inside your experience bullets.

BAD: "Built a web app. Skills: React, Node.js"
GOOD: "Architected a scalable web application using React and Node.js, decreasing load times by 40%."
