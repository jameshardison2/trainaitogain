Understanding the Outlier Assessment

The Outlier assessment is designed to test your strict adherence to instructions. Whether you are applying for the coding tier (Tier 3) or generalist tasks, you will be asked to evaluate two LLM responses. You must carefully review every constraint in the prompt.

Common Reasons for Failing

  • Ignoring Negative Constraints: If the prompt says "Do not use bullet points," and Model A gives a perfect answer but uses bullet points, it must be penalized.
  • Poor Justifications: When you write your rationale, you cannot just say "Model A is better." You must write a detailed, professional paragraph citing specific errors or hallucinations in Model B.
