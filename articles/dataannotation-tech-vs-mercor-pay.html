DataAnnotation Tech vs Mercor: Pay Rates Compared

When searching for remote AI training and RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) jobs, two of the biggest names that come up are DataAnnotation Tech and Mercor. Both offer highly flexible, remote work, but their pay structures and project availability differ significantly.

DataAnnotation Tech Pay Rates

DataAnnotation Tech typically starts generalists around $20 to $25 per hour. For coding and highly specialized domains (like mathematics or law), the pay can rise to $40+ per hour. The platform is known for its flexibility, allowing you to log on and work whenever projects are available on your dashboard.

Mercor Pay Rates

Mercor operates differently. Instead of an open dashboard, they match you directly with specific AI labs or startups for longer-term projects. Because of this targeted matching, Mercor's pay rates are generally higher, ranging from $40/hr for specialists up to $150/hr for elite software engineers and medical doctors.

Which Should You Choose?

DataAnnotation is excellent for supplemental income, but the onboarding process can be opaque, and many applicants report never hearing back after taking the initial assessment. Mercor (and similar platforms like Micro1) offer significantly higher ceilings for earning and faster onboarding if you target their active pipelines.
