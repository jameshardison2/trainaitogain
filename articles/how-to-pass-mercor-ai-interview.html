How to Pass the Mercor AI Evaluator Interview

The rise of generative AI has created a massive demand for human feedback, and companies like Mercor are at the forefront of hiring experts to evaluate and train these models. If you are preparing for the Mercor AI interview, understanding their specific evaluation criteria is critical to landing roles that pay between $40 and $150 per hour.

What is the Mercor AI Interview?

Unlike traditional technical interviews that focus on LeetCode or whiteboard problems, the Mercor interview is highly specialized. It is designed to test your ability to evaluate large language model (LLM) outputs for accuracy, safety, and domain-specific correctness. You will typically be presented with two model responses to a prompt and asked to determine which one is superior.

Core Competencies Tested

  • Factuality and Hallucination Detection: Can you identify when a model confidently states incorrect information? You must be able to fact-check complex domain-specific claims.
  • Instruction Following: Did the model actually answer the prompt? Many candidates fail because they prioritize eloquent writing over strict adherence to constraints (e.g., "Write a 3-sentence summary").
  • Formatting and Syntax: Especially in Software Engineering (SWE) and Medical pipelines, the formatting of code or clinical notes must be flawless.

How to Prepare and Pass

To succeed, you need to adopt the mindset of an auditor, not just a reader. Read the prompt first and identify every single constraint. Then, read both responses side-by-side. Look for subtle errors. When writing your justification for why Model A is better than Model B, be extremely specific. Do not say "Model A sounds better." Say "Model A correctly adhered to the 3-sentence constraint and accurately cited the API documentation, whereas Model B hallucinated a deprecated function call."
