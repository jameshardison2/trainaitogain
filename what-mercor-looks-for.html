Question 04

What Mercor actually looks for.

Mercor publishes the signals that raise your odds. Almost nobody applying has read them. Every item here is a fixable thing, most in under ten minutes.

The published list

From Mercor's own referrer documentation, top candidates typically have:

  • A complete Mercor profile and a strong résumé
  • A professional, up-to-date LinkedIn with clear credentials
  • A profile photo — and a GitHub link if it's relevant to your field
  • Camera enabled during the AI interview, which is required for verification
  • No VPN usage during the application process

Their stated reasoning: the more data points you provide, the more confident Mercor is when extending an offer.

The one that surprises people

Turn your VPN off. Running one during the application process counts against you. If you use a VPN by default — a lot of technical people do — this is worth checking before you apply, and it's invisible as a problem otherwise.
