The 4 Pillars of AI Speech Scoring

Both Mercor and micro1 evaluate your audio using speech-to-text models paired with LLM rubrics. Here is how your response is scored:

1. STAR Framework Structure

Does the candidate follow Situation, Task, Action, and Result? Unstructured stories get penalized for rambling.

2. Domain Terminology

Does the transcript contain specific technical and domain vocabulary matching the role specification?

3. Measurable Metrics

Are claims supported by quantifiable data (percentages, time saved, dollars generated, scale achieved)?

4. Pacing & Vocal Delivery

Steady cadence between 110-140 words per minute, free of long silences or repetitive conversational filler.
