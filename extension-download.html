How to Install (Developer Mode)

Because this tool interacts aggressively with Mercor's platform to protect your application, it must be installed locally.

1

Unzip the Folder

Find the downloaded get-you-hired-extension.zip file on your computer and extract it. You should now have a folder named extension.

2

Open Chrome Extensions

In your Chrome browser, type chrome://extensions into the URL bar and hit Enter.

3

Enable Developer Mode

In the top right corner of the Extensions page, toggle the switch that says "Developer mode" to the ON position.

4

Load Unpacked

Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top left. Select the unzipped extension folder. That's it! The extension is now active.

5

Pin The Extension

Click the puzzle piece icon (🧩) in the top right of your Chrome browser, and click the pin icon next to "TrainAIToGain" so you can easily access your tools during the application.
