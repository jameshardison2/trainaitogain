Waiting-Stage Explainers: What Comes Next

Candidate forums are full of anxiety about "under review" statuses. Here is the exact breakdown of how Mercor and micro1 evaluate you after you apply.

⚡

Mercor: "Under Review" & 3-Min Video

What it means: Your resume passed the initial semantic vector screening. When an enterprise lab starts a wave, top candidates get an invitation for an asynchronous 3-minute camera-on assessment.

Pro Tip: Keep answers strictly within 60–90 seconds using first-person ownership ("I architected", not "We"). Never pause longer than 5 seconds without saying a hold-the-floor line.
🌐

micro1: Hiring Manager Review (HMR)

What it means: You completed the Zara AI interview. Your score has entered the internal HMR pool where project managers match clients with evaluated contractors.

Critical Rule: Ensure your profile availability is set to 20–40 hrs/wk. Selecting 10 hours for a 20-hour role permanently excludes you from project allocations.
💳

Getting Paid: Okta & Deel Provisioning

Onboarding flow: Once selected, you will receive an enterprise invitation to Okta (for secure SSO project access) and Deel (for international/US contractor payroll).

Payroll Cadence: Payouts occur weekly or bi-weekly via Deel direct deposit or wire transfer in USD or local currency. Upload your tax form (W-8BEN/W-9) on Day 1.
📚

Official Policies & Direct Portals

Avoid outdated forum rumors. Both platforms maintain authoritative documentation for contractor policies, payment calendars, and platform compliance.
