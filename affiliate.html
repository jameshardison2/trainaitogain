Understanding the Opportunity

What Is a Mercor Hiring Wave?

🤖 AI Labs Need Human Experts

Major AI companies contract Mercor to source real-world professionals - doctors, engineers, accountants - to evaluate and correct their AI outputs. When a new contract starts, Mercor opens hundreds of evaluator roles simultaneously.

🌊 A Wave = High Demand Period

When many roles open at once and hiring is fast, that's a wave. During a wave, getting hired takes days - not weeks. Work flows immediately after onboarding. Waves close when contracts are staffed, so timing matters. Right now is a wave.

⚡ Waves Don't Last Forever

Once enough evaluators are hired, Mercor pauses the role. The badge system on their site tells you what stage each role is in. No badge = apply now. "Closing soon" = post with urgency. "Submitted" = skip completely.

For Affiliates

6 Ways to Convert More on an Active Wave

01

Lead with the pay rate in your first line

Don't bury it. "$75–100/hr reviewing AI outputs in your field" stops the scroll immediately. Put the number first, the explanation second.

02

Use wave urgency, not generic FOMO

"935 people hired this month - wave is open now" is specific. Vague "limited spots available" gets ignored. Real numbers create real urgency.

03

Post Tue–Thu, 8–10am or 12–2pm

LinkedIn engagement peaks mid-week during professional hours. A post at 9am Tuesday outperforms the same post on Sunday evening 3× on average.

04

Match the role to the group's identity

Don't post AML roles in a general business group. Post AML in compliance groups, civil engineering in ASCE communities. Relevance = trust = conversion.

05

Include the hired count as social proof

"935 people already hired" is more convincing than "apply now." It signals the opportunity is real, vetted, and moving - not a scam or a new unproven thing.

06

End every post with one clear CTA

One link, one action: "Apply here ➔ [your referral link]". Multiple links or options kill conversion. Make it effortless to take the next step.
