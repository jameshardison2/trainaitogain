Phase 01 · Resume Calibration ⏱️ Estimated: 5 Minutes

The "Keyword Context" Strategy

AI applicant screeners do not read like humans. They run semantic extraction algorithms that weigh terms embedded directly inside accomplishments far higher than isolated skill lists at the bottom of a page.

❌ Weak Skill List (Low Score)

"Built internal tools and worked on database tasks. Skills: Python, SQL, REST APIs, Git."

Parser verdict: Passive construction, unquantified impact, disconnected skills dump.
✅ Embedded Context (High Score)

"Architected an automated data ingestion pipeline using Python and SQL, decreasing model evaluation turnaround by 42% across 10,000 weekly samples."

Parser verdict: Clear action verb, domain keywords in context, concrete scale and percentage.

High-Signal Action Verbs to Lead Every Bullet:

Ownership: Spearheaded, Directed, Architected, Refactored, Evaluated, Benchmarked.
Execution: Engineered, Deployed, Calibrated, Validated, Fine-tuned, Audited.
Measurable Impact: Accelerated, Increased, Reduced, Compressed, Optimized.

Phase 02 · One-Way Screening ⏱️ Estimated: 10 Minutes

The Spoken STAR Impact Formula

During automated video screens on Mercor or micro1 (Zara), speech-to-text models transcribe your voice in real time. If your answers wander without structure, your score drops. Target 60 to 90 seconds per answer using this exact timing formula:

10 SECONDS
Situation: "In my most recent role at [Company/Project], our team was tasked with [Specific Objective or Bottleneck]."
10 SECONDS
Task: "My direct ownership was to [Clear Measurable Goal] before the end of the quarter."
35 SECONDS
Action: "I spearheaded the implementation of [System/Methodology], specifically leveraging [Key Domain Skill] to resolve edge-case failures."
15 SECONDS
Result: "As a direct result, we achieved [Quantifiable Metric: e.g. 38% latency reduction or $50K savings], exceeding our target."
10 SECONDS
Tie-In: "This experience prepared me directly to evaluate complex prompts with high precision in this role."

Phase 03 · Environment Calibration ⏱️ Estimated: 5 Minutes

Technical Setup & Retake Rules

Both Mercor and micro1 enforce strict technical protocols. Minor issues like browser incompatibilities or poor microphone levels can trigger automatic screening failures before you finish.

🌐 Browser & WebRTC
Google Chrome is strongly recommended for optimal audio/video capture and WebRTC stability across all partner portals.
🎙️ Audio & Lighting
A dedicated wired or USB headset prevents room echo. Face natural light or a lamp directly; avoid backlighting.
🔄 Platform Retake Rules
Mercor: Free practice available on work.mercor.com. Live screening retakes vary by client opening.
micro1: Test your video and audio with Zara before recording your final submission.

Phase 04 · Targeted Wave Application ⏱️ Estimated: Ongoing

Active Waves & Post-Hire Quality

Partner contract waves move rapidly as client project quotas fill. Once accepted, maintaining contract longevity requires rigorous quality standards:

  • Fact-Checking Sensitivity: Spot hallucinations accurately by citing authoritative external documentation and primary source links.
  • Evidence-Based Justifications: Write concise, rubric-backed rationales explaining exactly why a prompt response failed or succeeded.
  • Consistent Quality: Taking appropriate time on every task protects your accuracy rating and ensures ongoing project assignments.
