# Email Reply Templates

**The rule that matters more than any template: reply within 24 hours, personally.**

Both of your hires came from real back-and-forth. A same-day reply from someone who clearly knows
the platform converts far better than any automated sequence. These are starting points, not
scripts — change them to fit what the person actually said.

## What not to do

- **Don't attach the PDF cold.** It gets ignored. Offer it, send it when they say yes.
- **Don't promise placement.** Most applicants aren't placed. Saying otherwise is false and it's
  exactly what makes people distrust referral outreach.
- **Don't skip the disclosure.** FTC requirement, and with this audience it reads as a trust
  signal rather than a liability.
- **Don't let it sit.** A reply three days later converts far worse than one the same day.

---

## If you ever move to automated sequences

CAN-SPAM applies to commercial email: you need a physical mailing address in the footer and a
working one-click unsubscribe. Personal replies to someone who wrote to you first don't trigger
this. A marketing sequence to a list does.

Given your volume, manual replies are both higher-converting and simpler. Revisit automation only
if you're getting more submissions than you can personally answer.
