Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

TrainAIToGain ("we", "our", or "us") provides candidate preparation resources, interactive tools, and referral links for remote AI evaluation and RLHF positions. We are committed to transparency and respect for candidate privacy.

2. Resume Data & Privacy

When you use our Interactive ATS Resume Scanner or upload a resume to match against active hiring waves:

  • Your resume file is parsed client-side in your web browser.
  • Text extraction and keyword matching occur within your browser session or are transmitted securely to our AI inference endpoint strictly for generating keyword alignment scores.
  • We never sell, rent, or monetize your resume text or personal details.
  • Resume files are not permanently stored on our servers without your explicit consent.

3. Email Communications & 1-Click Unsubscribe

If you voluntarily sign up for our Weekly Role Alerts or Application Companion Guides:

  • We collect your email address solely to send the requested hiring alerts, blueprints, or status reminders.
  • Every email sent contains a clear, 1-click unsubscribe link. You can also opt out at any time by visiting /unsubscribe.
  • We do not share your email with third-party marketers.

4. Cookies & Tracking Choices

We respect your privacy choices under global data protection laws (including GDPR and CCPA/CPRA):

  • Opt-in by default: We do not load Google Analytics or tracking scripts until you click "Accept" on our Cookie Consent banner.
  • Decline honored: If you click "Decline", analytics scripts and pipeline telemetry remain disabled for your session.
  • Functional data (such as saved bookmarked roles and interview progress) is stored locally on your device in your browser's localStorage.

5. Third-Party Links & Referrals

Our site includes direct referral links to third-party hiring platforms (such as Mercor and micro1). When you click an external link to apply, you are directed to that provider's domain, and their terms and privacy policies govern their application process.

6. Data Rights & Contact

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of any personal data you have submitted to TrainAIToGain. To submit a request or inquire about our privacy practices, contact us:
