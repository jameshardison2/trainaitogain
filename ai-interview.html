Before you start (8)
  1. Device & browser: Best on a computer in Chrome or Edge. On a phone, use typed mode.
  2. Microphone & audio: Enable microphone permissions and use headphones with a mic to prevent echo.
  3. Quiet, well-lit space: Sit in a quiet room with a light source in front of you.
  4. Uninterrupted time: Set aside 15–20 uninterrupted minutes with pen and paper nearby.
  5. Know your resume: Prepare 2–3 past projects with specific metrics using the STAR method.
  6. Steady pacing: Speak naturally and aim for 60–90 seconds per answer.
  7. Mercor practice: Mercor offers practice interviews on its platform.
  8. micro1 prep: Pick one core skill for your profile and use micro1's Interview Prep tool to test your video and audio.
