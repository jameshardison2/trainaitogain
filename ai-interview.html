📋 Official AI Interview Readiness Checklist (Mercor & micro1)
  • Use a computer, not a phone: Mercor does not support mobile browsers. Full-screen sharing is required.
  • Chrome browser: Camera and microphone enabled. Chrome is recommended for optimal audio/video WebRTC stability.
  • Quiet room, face visible: Good lighting in front of your face. Use headphones with a mic to eliminate room echo.
  • Block 20 uninterrupted minutes: Have paper and pen ready for notes.
  • Know your resume line by line: The AI asks directly about your past experience. Have 2 or 3 short stories with quantified results.
  • Speak naturally: Don't read from a script; eye contact with the camera looks confident.
  • Mercor practice rule: Use Mercor's free, unlimited practice interview first! Real screening submissions attach directly to your candidate profile for client review.
  • micro1 AI interview: Pick one core skill for your profile; use the Interview Prep tool to test your video and audio.
